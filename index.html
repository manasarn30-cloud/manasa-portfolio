01 — About

A closer look at my background and how I work.

01 / Approach

Build first, refine later.

I learn concepts fastest by wiring them up and watching them work — or fail. My working style is action-oriented and output-first. I'd rather have a working prototype to iterate on than a plan sitting on paper.

02 / Signal-aware

Bridging the gap.

Comfortable moving between physical sensors and the math that makes sense of their data.

03 / Education

Academic Grounding

B.E. Electronics & Communication Engineering at Kumaraguru College of Technology. Coursework heavily centers on Embedded Systems and Cyber-Physical Systems.

04 / Systems Thinking

The Bigger Picture

I am drawn to problems that live at the boundary: reading a sensor correctly, filtering noisy signals, and making a system respond in real time. Interested in how embedded components behave as part of a larger, connected system.
